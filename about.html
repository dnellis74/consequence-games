Builder's Statement

I'm David Nellis, a software engineer in Salida, Colorado. I learned what computers were for on an Apple II, mapping dungeons in The Bard's Tale and Might and Magic on graph paper, and I have been building small things ever since.

Consequence Games is where I put the games I make. The rule for this site is that your time is worth more than my numbers. In practice that means:

  • No accounts and no ads.
  • Every game loads fast and starts with one tap.
  • A round is short and ends clearly. When you stop, you lose nothing.
  • No streaks to protect, no timers that call you back, and nothing that rewards you for staying longer than you meant to.

The name comes from the idea I find most interesting in games and in economics: every choice has a cost, and the cost often arrives later. The games here are small, but they try to make that cost visible.

If one of them takes a minute of your day, I want that minute to have been worth it.
