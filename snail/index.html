Where this came from

In 2014, on the Rooster Teeth podcast, Gavin Free asked a question: would you take $10 million and immortality if an immortal snail followed you for the rest of your life, and its touch killed you? It turned into a meme, the immortal snail, also called the snail assassin.

Snails were chasing people long before that. In the margins of 13th and 14th century manuscripts, scribes drew armored knights fighting snails, and the knights were often losing. One theory, from the scholar Lilian Randall, is that the snails stood for Lombard moneylenders.

This game puts the two together. Money piles up while you run, and everything you pick up makes you slower. The snail never speeds up. It just never stops, and its trail never dries.

Blob v Snail is a variant of Blob, my touchscreen take on Snake.
