Links

Other corners of the web.

Sites I like that respect your time, in no particular order except the first one.

  • neal.fun · Neal Agarwal's web games and interactive experiments. The site that got me started on this one.
  • R74n · A collective of browser projects, including Sandboxels, a falling-sand simulator you can lose an afternoon in.
  • Bartosz Ciechanowski · Interactive articles about physics, math, and engineering: watches, cameras, bicycles, gears.
  • Nicky Case · Explorable explanations, including The Evolution of Trust, an interactive guide to the game theory of why we trust each other.
  • Red Blob Games · Amit Patel's interactive explanations of math and algorithms for games. His hexagonal grids guide is the standard reference.
  • Universal Paperclips · Frank Lantz's 2017 incremental game. You are an AI that makes paperclips.
  • Radio Garden · Spin a globe and listen to live radio stations wherever you stop.
  • Pointer Pointer · Hold your mouse still and a photo appears of someone pointing at it. Desktop only.

Know one that belongs here? Send it to me.
